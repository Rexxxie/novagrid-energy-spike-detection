How to read this

  • Spike = a reading where energy_usage_kWh > 1.4 × baseline_kWh — NovaGrid's own documented rule, re-derived here from the raw readings rather than trusted from the spike_detected column.
  • Blind readings are readings where 1.4 × baseline is higher than the largest reading any meter in the fleet has ever produced. The rule cannot fire on them at any consumption level.
  • Wasted energy is the reading minus its own baseline, priced at the Ofgem Jan–Mar 2024 electricity unit rate of 28.62 p/kWh. It is what the household paid for above its own normal pattern — not a full bill impact.
  • Every chart has a Table toggle carrying the same numbers, and all charts respond to the one filter row above.
